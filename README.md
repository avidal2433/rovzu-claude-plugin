# Rovzu for Claude

Rovzu is a reading library for the articles, videos and posts you save to read later. This plugin connects your Rovzu library to Claude and teaches Claude how to use it well.

## What you can do

- Search what you saved by the words in its title, site or text.
- Read an article in Rovzu's reading view at full screen, inside the conversation, and ask about a passage you select.
- Ask for a summary, a comparison or a quote from an article in your library.
- Save up to 10 links at once, and mark items as finished, archive them or bring them back to your pending list.

Nothing can be deleted from the conversation, and while you study an article in Rovzu its text is never shared.

## Use it

After adding the plugin, connect Rovzu from the plugin's **Connectors** tab and sign in to your Rovzu account, or create one. Then ask, for example, "What have I saved about productivity?" or "Open the article about memory so I can read it". More at https://rovzu.com/asistentes.

## Data

The plugin's skill runs no code. Its connector talks to Rovzu's server at https://rovzu.com/mcp, which sends Claude only what each request needs: the titles, addresses and lists of the items it finds, and the text of the articles it reads. Rovzu's privacy policy is at https://rovzu.com/privacidad.
